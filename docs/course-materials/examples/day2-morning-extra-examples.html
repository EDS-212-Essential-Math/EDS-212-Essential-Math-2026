Limits

  1. Evaluate: \[ \lim_{x\to 3}(2x^2-5x+1) \]
TipSolution

\[ \lim_{x\to 3}(2x^2-5x+1) = 2(3)^2-5(3)+1 = 18-15+1 = 4 \]

  1. Does the following limit exist? \[ \lim_{x\to 1}\frac{|x-1|}{x-1} \]
TipSolution

Approaching from the right (\(x>1\)): \(\frac{x-1}{x-1}=1\).

Approaching from the left (\(x<1\)): \(\frac{-(x-1)}{x-1}=-1\).

Since the function approaches different values from the left and right, the limit does not exist.

  1. Evaluate: \[ \lim_{x\to -2}(x^3+4x-1) \]
TipSolution

\[ \lim_{x\to -2}(x^3+4x-1) = (-2)^3+4(-2)-1 = -8-8-1 = -17 \]

  1. Let \[ f(x)=\begin{cases}x+1, & x\geq 0\\-x+1, & x<0\end{cases} \] Does the following limit exist? \[ \lim_{x\to 0}f(x) \]
TipSolution

Approaching from the right (\(x\geq 0\)): \(f(x)=x+1 \to 1\).

Approaching from the left (\(x<0\)): \(f(x)=-x+1 \to 1\).

Since the function approaches the same value from both sides, the limit exists and equals \(1\).

Rules for differentiation

  1. Find \(f'(x)\) for \(f(x)=x^5\)
TipSolution

\[ f'(x)=5x^4 \]

  1. Find \(f'(x)\) for \(f(x)=\frac{1}{x^2}\)
TipSolution

\[ f(x)=x^{-2} \Rightarrow f'(x)=-2x^{-3}=-\frac{2}{x^3} \]

  1. Find \(y'\) for \(y=x^3-x^2-15\)
TipSolution

\[ y'=3x^2-2x \]

  1. Find \(\frac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y=x^2+\sqrt{2}x-\frac{8}{x}+4\)
TipSolution

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=2x+\sqrt{2}+\frac{8}{x^2} \]

  1. Find \(g'(y)\) for \(g(y)=2\sqrt{y}-e^y+20\ln(y)\)
TipSolution

\[ g'(y)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{y}}-e^y+\frac{20}{y} \]

Higher-order derivatives

  1. Find the third derivative of \(H(z)=2z^5-4z^3+6z-7\)
TipSolution

\[ H'(z)=10z^4-12z^2+6 \] \[ H''(z)=40z^3-24z \] \[ H'''(z)=120z^2-24 \]

  1. Position is given by \(s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t\). Find the velocity function \(s'(t)\) and the acceleration function \(s''(t)\).
TipSolution

\[ v(t)=s'(t)=3t^2-12t+9 \] \[ a(t)=s''(t)=6t-12 \]

Partial derivatives

  1. Find \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\) and \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\) for \(f(x,y)=x^2y-2x+y-1\), then evaluate both at \((1,3)\).
TipSolution

\[ \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=2xy-2, \qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}=x^2+1 \] At \((1,3)\): \(\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,3)}=2(1)(3)-2=4\), and \(\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,3)}=1^2+1=2\)

  1. Find \(\frac{\partial B}{\partial x}\), \(\frac{\partial B}{\partial y}\), and \(\frac{\partial B}{\partial z}\) for \(B(x,y,z)=0.4x^3y-3.6y^2+4zx+8\)
TipSolution

\[ \frac{\partial B}{\partial x}=1.2x^2y+4z, \qquad \frac{\partial B}{\partial y}=0.4x^3-7.2y, \qquad \frac{\partial B}{\partial z}=4x \]
